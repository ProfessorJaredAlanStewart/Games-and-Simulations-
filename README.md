# Government Games & Simulations

**Free, browser-based games and simulations for American Government and Texas Government courses.** No logins, no installations, no cost — just open and play.

🔗 **Live site:** https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/

Released as an Open Educational Resource and designed to pair with the [Civic Action Project (CAP)](https://professorjaredalanstewart.github.io/Civic-Action-Project/).

----

## What this is

Every resource here is a **single, self-contained HTML file** that runs entirely in the browser — no accounts, no servers, no setup. Project one on a screen for a review session, assign it as homework, or hand a student the link. They all work the same way: click and go.

## Resources

### 🥾 Trailblazer Treks
Self-paced interactive learning modules with built-in checkpoints and progress tracking. *(Hosted in companion repositories.)*

- [Federal Government Trailblazer Trek](https://professorjaredalanstewart.github.io/tcc-govt-modules/) — a guided trek through the Constitution, the branches, civil liberties, and public policy.
- [Texas Government Trailblazer Trek](https://professorjaredalanstewart.github.io/texas-govt-modules/) — the Texas Constitution, the legislature, the executive branch, and local government.

### 📝 Study & Review Tools
Generators and games for reviewing key concepts before exams or as in-class activities.

- [Federal Government Crossword Generator](https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/Federal%20Government%20Crossword%20Generator.html) — randomized crossword puzzles on federal government concepts.
- [Texas Government Crossword Generator](https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/Texas%20Government%20Crossword%20Generator.html) — randomized crosswords on Texas government topics.
- [Texas Government Quiz Builder](https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/Texas%20Government%20Quiz%20Builder.html) — build and take custom quizzes for exam prep or quick knowledge checks.
- [Texas Jeopardy — Classroom Edition](https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/Texas%20Jeopardy%20Classroom%20Edition.html) — a full Jeopardy-style board for team-based review.
- [Wheel of Fortune — Texas Government](https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/Wheel_of_Fortune_Texas_Government.html) — spin and solve Texas government puzzles.
- [Wheel of Fortune — Federal Government](https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/Wheel_of_Fortune_Federal_Government.html) — spin and solve federal government puzzles.

### 📚 Research & Writing
Tools that help students do the scholarly work — finding sources, citing them correctly, and writing about them.

- [Annotated Bibliography Desk](https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/annotated-bibliography.html) — drop in a PDF or Word article and it pulls a verified APA 7 citation from CrossRef, guides the annotation with a writing scaffold, and exports to Word, RIS, BibTeX, or print. Fully self-contained and works offline.

### 🛠 Instructor Tools
Utilities for course planning and organization.

- [TCC Syllabus Generator](https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/TCC%20Syllabus%20Generator.html) — a step-by-step syllabus builder that outputs a print-ready PDF with required institutional sections.
- [Generic Schedule Builder](https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/Generic%20Schedule%20Builder%20%28For%20Dist%20to%20Chairs%29.html) — a flexible schedule builder for department chairs and lead instructors.

### 🏛 Simulations
Role-playing activities where students experience legislative processes firsthand.

- [Keep Your Job — Congressional Simulation](https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/Keep%20Your%20Job%20-%20Congressional%20Simulation%20Game.html) — play as a member of Congress navigating committees, floor votes, constituents, and reelection.
- [Keep Your Seat — Texas State Senate Simulation](https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/Keep%20Your%20Seat%20-%20Texas%20State%20Senate%20Simulation.html) — serve as a Texas State Senator balancing constituents, party, and policy.
- [Presidential Primary Game](https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/Presidential%20Primary%20Game.html) — campaign through the primary process, making calls on messaging, fundraising, and state-by-state strategy.

### 🎲 Bonus — Just for Fun
- [Wheel of Levi](https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/Wheel_of_Levi.html) — a wacky custom Wheel of Fortune made for one very specific kid. Not educational. Not serious. Purely chaotic.

## Using the resources

Open the [live site](https://jaredalanstewart.com/Games-and-Simulations-/) and click any card, or open an individual HTML file directly in any modern browser. Each one runs locally on the device — nothing is uploaded to a server.

## Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This collection is published with GitHub Pages. To host your own copy:

1. Put the HTML files and `index.html` in the repository.
2. Go to **Settings → Pages**.
3. Under **Build and deployment**, set **Source** to *Deploy from a branch*, choose the `main` branch and the `/ (root)` folder, and save.
4. The site goes live at `https://<username>.github.io/<repo>/` (or your custom domain).

## Built with

Most resources are hand-built HTML/CSS/JavaScript with no dependencies. The **Annotated Bibliography Desk** bundles a few open-source libraries directly in its single file:

- [pdf.js](https://mozilla.github.io/pdf.js/) — Mozilla, Apache License 2.0 (PDF text extraction)
- [Mammoth.js](https://github.com/mwilliamson/mammoth.js) — BSD 2-Clause (Word `.docx` text extraction)
- [Spectral](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Spectral) and [Inter](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter) — SIL Open Font License 1.1
- Citation data from the [CrossRef REST API](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/rest-api/)
