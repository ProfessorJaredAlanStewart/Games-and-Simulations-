Time & Effort Log Builder

Answer a few questions about how your chair work usually goes, and this page will create your Department Chair Time & Effort Tracker for 2026–2027 with the hours already entered. You can still change any day in the tracker afterward.

About you

Use the dates your chair duties actually ran, not the calendar months. Fall must fall between Aug 1 and Dec 31, Spring between Jan 1 and May 15, Summer between May 16 and Aug 31; that is how the tracker file is laid out.

A typical week

About how many hours of chair work you do on each day of a normal week. Teaching days are usually one or two hours; office days more. Put 0 on days you never do chair work.

100 means the same, 50 means half as many hours. Only matters if Summer 2027 is checked.
Sick days, travel, days that got away. 4 is a reasonable guess.
What the hours are spent on

Roughly what share of your chair time goes to each category. The six numbers need to add up to 100. If you type a few typical tasks under a category (one per line), some days in the tracker will show one of them as a note. Leave the boxes empty and the tracker will just have hours.

Busier stretches

Stretches that were much busier than normal: Connections Week, program review, the scheduling crunch, an accreditation visit. Enter the hours you put in per day. If that work leaned toward certain categories, put larger numbers under those categories and smaller or blank under the rest; only the relative sizes matter. Leave every category blank to use your normal split. Hours you enter here are kept exactly as typed.

Days off

Holidays, breaks, and leave. No hours will be entered on these dates. A guess at the TCC calendar is filled in; please check it against the official one.

Lighter stretches

Stretches where you were working, but less than usual, such as the week before classes start. Enter how much of a normal day you were putting in (50 means about half).

How the hours should look

Real weeks are never identical. A normal amount of variation makes the log look like it was kept by hand.

Don't like how the days came out? Click the button for a different arrangement of the same totals. Keep the mix number if you want to reproduce this exact log later.

The nudge only changes normal days; hours you typed under busier stretches are left alone. If the total on the right is still far off, change the hours in "A typical week" instead.

Projected log

This updates as you type. These are the totals the tracker file will show.

Open the downloaded tracker in any web browser; no internet needed. You can change any day's hours there. Changes are remembered on that computer, and the tracker has its own export button for turning in your log.
