Why this exists. Students notice cancelled classes, and the college is asked about them. One short report per cancelled session gives the department a complete record: what happened, how students were told, and what they worked on instead.

This is also how the department looks out for you. The reason you give is seen only by the chair and dean. If a pattern suggests you could use support — a medical accommodation, a schedule change, help with a commute or caregiving — the chair's job is to connect you with those resources, not to count against you. Keep medical details out of this form; “Sick” is enough. Anything FMLA-related goes through Human Resources.

What the Faculty Handbook requires when you miss class
  • Full-time faculty submit a request in the leave management system — before the absence when possible, otherwise immediately on return.
  • The department chair (or, in the chair's absence, the dean or VPAA) must be notified by phone or other means that you cannot meet class.
  • A class may not meet unsupervised, and students may not be left without notice of your absence.

This form records that each of those happened; it does not replace the leave request.

      Suggested reporting process

      1. Before class, if at all possible: call or text the chair (dean or VPAA if the chair is unreachable). Full-time faculty submit the leave request in the leave management system; if that can't happen first, do it immediately on return.
      2. Tell students the same way every time: a Canvas announcement with the word “cancelled,” the replacement assignment, and its due date. Ask the front desk to post a door sign for in-person sections.
      3. Give the session's work, not busywork: the assignment should carry the day's outcome and take roughly the class period. Online discussions, a short analysis, a recorded lecture with a response, or a Don't Cancel That Class workshop all qualify.
      4. Within two business days: complete the Report a cancellation tab, download the report file, and email it to the chair. One report per section affected.
      5. Chair, monthly: import reports into the log, review flags (missing leave request, late notice, repeat sections, no artifacts), check in with anyone who asked for outreach, and send the dean the CSV or printed summary.
      6. Dean, each term: compare cancellations per section across departments; use the reason mix to decide where resources (sub pools, DCTC capacity, flexible modality) would reduce cancellations.

      Don't Cancel That Class

      How the program works where it exists. A faculty member who knows in advance that they'll miss a session asks Student Affairs (often the Dean of Students office, counseling, the library, or career services) to send a presenter during the regular class time. Students still meet, attendance still counts, and the session covers something they need anyway: study and test-taking strategies, research and citation, résumé and career planning, financial literacy, wellness and stress, Title IX and bystander awareness, transfer planning. Programs typically ask for one to two weeks' notice and a short request form.

      What the Faculty Handbook says

      Faculty Absence from Class. A full-time faculty member who must miss class shall be required to submit a request in the current leave management system. A faculty member's absence must be approved by the appropriate divisional dean and by the vice president of academic affairs before the absence occurs, if at all possible. If the situation prevents the submission of the leave request before the absence occurs, then the leave request shall be submitted immediately after the faculty member's return to the campus. In any event, the department chair (or, in his or her absence, the divisional dean or the vice president of academic affairs) shall be notified by telephone or by other means that the instructor will not be able to meet classes.

      An instructor shall not allow a class to meet without supervision or without notification of the absence of the instructor.
