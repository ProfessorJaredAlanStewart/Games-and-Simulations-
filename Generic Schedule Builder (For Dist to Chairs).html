How placement works
  • Drag a section to the grid, or click it once and then click a time slot.
  • No scrolling: “Add section” carries its own Days and Time dropdowns, so a new section can go straight onto the grid. For sections already in the pool, click → on the card (or select it and press P). Slots already taken by that instructor are disabled.
  • With “Keep a spare after placing” on, placing a section leaves an identical unplaced one in that instructor's box, so repeat sections need no extra clicks. The spare stops appearing once the instructor reaches their max load.
  • Monday–Thursday sections auto-pair on M/W or T/Th. Blended, Friday, and Saturday sections meet once.
  • Labs are separate hatched LAB cards. A 2-slot lab fills two adjacent Monday–Thursday rows (the second shows “cont.”); Friday and Saturday blocks are already long enough, so a lab there takes one row. The 8:00–9:20 PM row exists so evening labs can run 6:30–9:20.
  • A lecture that feeds several lab sections (one 48-seat lecture, two 24-seat labs) is one lecture card marked “×2 labs”; place two lab cards for it. The needs panel flags courses short on labs.
  • Load is in load hours (handbook p. 53: one lecture hour = one lab hour = one contact hour). Set each course's lecture and lab hours in Setup. Full-time contract load is 15; the tool warns at the 6-hour overload cap and the 24-hour maximum.
  • Once-a-week evening lectures (6:30–9:15) still occupy a single 80-minute row; the tool approximates them.
  • Early and late half-term sections (8-week, 7-week) may share a slot; anything else double-booking an instructor is blocked.
  • Click the × on a placed section to return it to the pool. Everything autosaves in this browser.
